Busted out the Vita today and started playing Tearaway, what an amazing game! The art style and music give such a welcoming atmosphere. The gameplay mechanics use the unique features of the Vita so well. For example, you break through the game world by touching your fingers to the back touchpad and your face makes frequent appearances in the world (and story) thanks to the cameras. All of this is bundled in a fun, low-stakes game that makes you smile!

![Tearaway running on a vita](/content/images/tearaway-is-an-amazing-cozy-game/IMG_1573.webp) _Tearaway on my Vita_

If you have a Vita, I highly recommend giving this a play! If you don't have a Vita, unfortunately this is not a game to emulate. It's use of the unique hardware features of the Vita would translate poorly on any other device.
