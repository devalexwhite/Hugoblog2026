I've been playing around with the new release of [Omarchy Quattro](https://omarchy.org/) on my ThinkPad and have been enjoying it quite a bit!

![screenshot-2026-08-25\_13-30-54](/content/images/giving-omarchy-a-shot/screenshot-2026-08-25_13-30-54.webp)

The single biggest improvement over past releases of Omarchy has been the integration of AI agents with the system. Thanks to this integration, configuring Arch, Hyprland, etc no longer requires shifting through fragmented documentation and JSON/TOML config files. For example, I wanted to enable natural scrolling on my tochpad: instead of finding the Hyprland config and researching what setting I needed, I just pressed "Super + Ctrl + Shift + A" to open Claude and asked it to enable natural scroll.

There's also an integration with crash reporting in Omarchy. If something goes wrong, you get a notification that, when clicked, starts triaging the issue via your preferred LLM. Very useful!

I feel like this is a perfect use case for LLMs, making historically complex systems (Arch, Hyprland) accessible to anyone. It's a very cool, and different, way to interact with an OS.
