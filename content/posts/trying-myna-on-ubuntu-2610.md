A new, experimental feature in Ubuntu is [Myna](https://ubuntu.com/blog/myna-speech-to-text-technology-preview-available), a local speech-to-text engine. After giving it a shot, I can say it works really well, and feels native to the system. I've tried a few alternatives on Linux, and none of them have been as smooth or responsive as Myna. Everything from the on screen display as it transcribes, to the little "Ping" sound oozes polish.

I highly recommend giving it a shot!
