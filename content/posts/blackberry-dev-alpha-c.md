I was digging through a drawer and remembered I had this guy from college, the BlackBerry Dev Alpha C! It's a cool little device that was handed out to developers during the early days of BlackBerry 10. If you created a certain amount of BlackBerry apps, you'd be eligible for a device (and some cash)! I also got a BlackBerry Playbook for free. To be honest, neither were good devices. I remember the tablet was slow and clunky, and this guy had the worst camera you've ever seen (even back then).

![PXL\_20261024\_204504418](/content/images/blackberry-dev-alpha-c/PXL_20261024_204504418.webp)

Unfortunately I can't get past the splash screen, the device refuses to power up without a battery (which is long gone). Still, it's a neat relic and probably decently rare at this point!
