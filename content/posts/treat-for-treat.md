Yesterday was "[Trick or Treat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trick-or-treating)" in our neighborhood, so I thought it would be fun to make a little experiment to on this site celebrate Halloween. I'm calling it "Treat for Treat"!

The idea is simple, leave a treat (a link to your website or a site you love) and get a treat in return. 

> **Please keep submissions clean!** 
>
> No advertising or adult content. Links are reader submitted, so be aware you might get anything (this blog is not responsible for links submitted by others). If you get an inappropriate link, please report it.
