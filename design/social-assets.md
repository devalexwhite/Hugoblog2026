## Original banner prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a finished social preview banner for Alex White personal journal, wide 1200:630 aspect ratio. Quiet elegant literary editorial design matching a blog about being a dad, cycling in Ohio, technology, books and slowing down. Warm ivory paper #f6f4ed, charcoal #292e29, muted jade #4c6b59 and small terracotta #aa4b3f accents. Left half: large beautifully typeset serif text exactly "Alex White", small spaced uppercase text above exactly "A PERSONAL JOURNAL", below in smaller readable type exactly "Life, technology & the slower roads.", and near bottom left exactly "thatalexguy.dev". Right half: restrained Chinese ink wash illustration of a road bicycle beside a rural trail, hazy jade hills, grasses and a small terracotta sun. Refined ink brush texture and generous negative space. Small red seal with cream Chinese characters 小 above 艾 near upper left above heading. All text safely inset at least 80 px. No mockup, no frame, no extra text, no watermark. Output a single finished flat banner.
